Open on Launch
Use Rollio mostly to meter? In Settings, set Open on Launch to Light Meter and the app starts there. Films stays the default.

Fixes
• The roll list shows the right shot count after you edit a roll
• Frames saved from the light meter are numbered correctly, even several in a row
• Editing a frame right after saving it no longer drops a location that was still being found
• Shutter speeds in CSV exports no longer turn into dates in Numbers, Excel or Sheets
• No more "Discard changes?" on a form you did not touch
• The review prompt no longer appears after editing a roll
